How to play

1. One player taps Create game and shares the code. The other enters it and taps Join.

2. Each of you picks a secret 5-digit number. Digits can repeat, and it can start with 0.

3. Take turns guessing. Who goes first is picked at random, then you alternate. You each get up to 8 guesses.

Green: right digit, right spot. Yellow: the digit is in the number, in another spot. Gray: not in the number (or not that many times).

4. First to crack the other's number wins. If you crack it first in a round, the other player still gets that round's turn to tie.

Emoji: swipe the emoji strip above the keypad and tap one to send it. Hold an emoji to keep sending until you let go.

Solo: tap Play solo to crack a random number on your own. You get 10 guesses.

For two-player games, keep the page open while you play. Both phones need internet, on any network.
