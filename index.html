How to play

1. One player taps Create game and shares the code. The other enters it and taps Join.

2. Each of you picks a secret 5-digit number. Digits can repeat, and it can start with 0.

3. Take turns guessing. Who goes first is picked at random, then you alternate. You each get up to 8 guesses.
